About

EVILLICA is an independent digital horror creator and visual artist exploring modern internet aesthetics through short-form cinematic storytelling. Her work blends elements of analog horror, liminal environments, puppetry, and retro-digital visual language to create surreal micro-narratives that feel both nostalgic and disorienting. Each project is developed as a complete personal production — from concept and script to filming, performance, editing, and final visual design. Working across video, animated loops, and still digital imagery, EVILLICA builds compact fictional worlds that merge contemporary internet culture with the emotional textures of forgotten media.
